Product overview

How it works

This system combines chest X-ray analysis with structured health signals to produce a simple, interpretable risk output. It is designed for insight, not diagnosis.

Chest X-ray analysis

Your image is resized and normalized before being passed through a ResNet18-based model that predicts disease probabilities.

A Grad-CAM overlay highlights regions the model focused on for interpretability only.

Risk scoring

Structured inputs like age, smoking history, and exposure are processed through a machine learning pipeline to estimate lung cancer risk.

Results are grouped into tiers with simple guidance.

Combined insight

Imaging signals and risk scores are merged into a single summary output using a lightweight fusion rule.

This provides a broader view but should always be interpreted with clinical context.

Educational / research prototype — not a medical device.
